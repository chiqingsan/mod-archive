AQUASENSE 1.1.0
Author / 作者: chiqingsan

ENGLISH
=======

Requirements:
- Probably Stolen Demo 0.46D
- MelonLoader

Installation:
1. Install MelonLoader and launch the game once.
2. Close the game.
3. Copy `AquaSense.dll` into the game's `Mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and load a save.

Typical path:
D:\app\steam\steamapps\common\Probably Stolen Demo\Mods\AquaSense.dll

Important:
- Keep only one AquaSense DLL in the Mods folder.
- The mod does not include MelonLoader or game files.
- If the game updates, this mod may require a rebuild.

What it adds:
- Scanner-gated water composition tooltip.
- Standard bottled water support.
- Original water-quality label after the purity value.
- Automatic label refresh after liquid transfer when a scanner is owned.
- Original game flow preserved when no scanner is owned.
- Filter name and durability shown in the tooltip, including empty containers.
- Automatic depleted-filter replacement across nested player containers.
- Advanced > vanilla > other compatible filter priority.
- Position, shape, boundary, and overlap checks before exchange.
- A bilingual notice after a successful replacement.

Settings:
Open the `AquaSense` category in MelonPreferences. Three switches, all on by default:
- Automatically replace depleted filters.
- Show filter durability in the tooltip.
- Refresh water labels after pouring.

Uninstall:
Close the game and delete `AquaSense.dll` from the Mods folder.


中文
====

依赖：
- Probably Stolen Demo 0.46D
- MelonLoader

安装：
1. 安装 MelonLoader，并启动游戏一次。
2. 关闭游戏。
3. 将 `AquaSense.dll` 复制到游戏 Mods 目录。
4. 启动游戏并载入存档。

常见路径：
D:\app\steam\steamapps\common\Probably Stolen Demo\Mods\AquaSense.dll

注意：
- Mods 目录中只保留一个 AquaSense DLL。
- 本 Mod 不包含 MelonLoader 或游戏文件。
- 游戏更新后，可能需要重新编译 Mod。

功能：
- 拥有水质扫描仪时显示水质成分。
- 支持标准瓶装水。
- 在纯度数值后显示游戏原有水质标签。
- 拥有扫描仪时，倒水后自动更新原版水质标签。
- 没有扫描仪时保持游戏原有逻辑，不强制刷新标签。
- 悬浮面板显示滤芯名称与耐久度，水罐为空时同样显示。
- 自动检索顶层库存和嵌套容器中的过滤器。
- 替换优先级为高级过滤器、普通过滤器、其他兼容过滤器。
- 交换前检查位置、形状、边界和重叠，无法安全交换时保持原状。
- 替换成功后弹出中英双语提示。

设置：
在 MelonPreferences 的 `AquaSense` 分类下，三项开关默认全部开启：
- 滤芯耗尽后自动更换。
- 悬浮面板显示滤芯耐久。
- 倒水后自动更新水质标签。

卸载：
关闭游戏后，从 Mods 目录删除 `AquaSense.dll`。
